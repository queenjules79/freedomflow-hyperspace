Where we can meet

Freedom Flow bodywork and radical life coaching can happen in person or on Zoom. Choose the setting that suits you, then email or call to arrange a time.

Rainbow's End, Woodland Wellbeing Centre

One place to meet in person is Rainbow's End, a timber tree-house at Woodland Wellbeing Centre. It sits in ancient Cornish woodland a couple of miles west of St Austell, held between the oaks.

The room is quiet and warm: a treatment couch, a wood stove, and windows looking into the trees. It is a still place for bodywork, and for coaching that wants you in the room rather than on a screen. You might hear the woodland more than the road. The session can include the somatic protocols that listen to the body and help settle the nervous system.

The centre's postcode is PL26 7LU. Julianne is listed on the Woodland Wellbeing website with the other practitioners who work from the tree-house.

Online, via Zoom

If you would rather not travel, or you live too far to meet in person, sessions are available online via Zoom. They work across time zones, and you can stay in a place you already feel comfortable. The LaChiara method listens and adapts in the moment, so language-based coaching still moves when you are not in the same room. More on the LaChiara method.
